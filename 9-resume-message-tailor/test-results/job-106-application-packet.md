About GreenChoice
GreenChoice, PBC is a U.S.-based, leading provider of food health and sustainability data and personalization solutions for the grocery industry. GreenChoice gives food retailers instant access to personalized evidence-based food health scores & sustainability scores and verified attribute data on over 1 million food products, with award-winning shelf-edge and e-commerce plugins to meet consumers' changing health needs and values in-store and online. GreenChoice has guided over 10 million healthier purchases. Google named GreenChoice one of the three "Best Apps for Good" in the U.S. GreenChoice is backed by Katapult and BonBillo Ventures, works with Stanford University School of Medicine, is a member of Tufts Friedman School Food & Nutrition Innovation Council, an FMI Tech Finalist, and a Progressive Grocer GenNext Honoree. For more info about GreenChoice, visit: www.greenchoice.co

About the Role
As a Full Stack Engineer at GreenChoice, you will design, build, and maintain end-to-end solutions that power both our user-facing applications and backend systems. You’ll work across the stack—developing seamless web and mobile experiences with React.js and React Native, while also architecting APIs, services, and data pipelines with Node.js and modern databases.

This role will require strong collaboration with product, design, data, and infrastructure teams to deliver features that are scalable, secure, and high-performing. You will be a key contributor to shaping GreenChoice’s technical roadmap in a fast-paced, agile environment.

Key Responsibilities

Frontend Development:
- Build and maintain web applications using React.js and mobile applications using React Native with Expo.
- Translate Figma UI/UX designs into high-quality, pixel-perfect interfaces.
- Implement responsive design principles and ensure cross-platform compatibility.

Backend Development:
- Design, implement, and maintain RESTful and GraphQL APIs using Node.js/Express.
- Work with relational and NoSQL databases (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis) to model, query, and optimize data storage.
- Ensure data integrity, performance, and scalability across services.

Integration & Collaboration:
- Collaborate with frontend and backend teams to ensure seamless data flow and API integrations.
- Participate in code reviews, technical design discussions, and architectural decision-making.

Quality & Maintenance:
- Write unit and integration tests to ensure application stability.
- Maintain clean, well-documented, and reusable code.
- Debug, optimize, and monitor performance of applications across the stack.
